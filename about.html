About Our School

A supportive, vibrant and future-focused learning environment where students are encouraged to grow in confidence, character and capability.

Our identity

Learning with values, purpose and care

Anand Bal Vidya Mandir is committed to developing young people who are academically prepared, socially responsible and ready to contribute meaningfully to society.

Our curriculum blends classroom excellence with experience, sports, arts, technology and service opportunities that deepen each learner’s sense of belonging and personal growth.

Vision

To create a thriving learning community that inspires innovation, integrity and well-rounded achievement.

Mission

To nurture every child through quality teaching, compassion, creativity and a culture of excellence.

Values

Respect, curiosity, responsibility, inclusion and perseverance shape the school experience.
